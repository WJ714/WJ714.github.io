<span style="color: black;">About me</span>
======
I am a postdoctoral researcher at UC Berkeley (with Prof. Trevor Keenan) working at the intersection of eco-hydrology, the global carbon–water–energy cycle, and AI. I study how land-surface processes — evapotranspiration, sensible heat exchange, vegetation water stress, and disturbance-driven change — regulate the exchange of carbon, water, and energy between ecosystems and the atmosphere. My work integrates eddy-covariance flux observations, streamflow and remote-sensing data, interpretable machine learning, and Earth foundation models to constrain land-surface behavior from single flux towers to the contiguous US and the globe. Please find my full background on my *CV* and recent work under *publications* or on ResearchGate.

My current research spans three themes: (1) **land–atmosphere energy partitioning** and the energy-balance closure problem; (2) **wildfire disturbance and post-fire carbon recovery** across North America; and (3) **Earth foundation models** (OlmoEarth, Prithvi-EO-2, AlphaEarth) for high-resolution carbon and water flux mapping.
