About

I use machine learning and deep learning to understand the land’s water, carbon and energy cycles.

I combine flux towers, streamflow and satellite data with Earth foundation models, from single sites to the globe. Papers are on Google Scholar and ResearchGate.

Research

  • Deep learning and geo-foundation models for carbon and ET mapping

    I apply Earth foundation models such as Prithvi-EO-2 and AlphaEarth, pairing their satellite embeddings with flux-tower observations in deep learning models, to map carbon and evapotranspiration at high resolution from single towers to continents and the globe.

    • Deep learning
    • Earth foundation models
    • Prithvi-EO-2
    • AlphaEarth
    • AmeriFlux & NEON

  • Machine learning for evapotranspiration and hydrology

    How much water land surfaces return to the atmosphere, how evapotranspiration partitions into transpiration and evaporation, and how vegetation water stress shapes both, learned from flux, streamflow and remote-sensing data with interpretable machine learning.

    • ET partitioning
    • Interpretable ML
    • Water stress
    • Streamflow

  • Surface energy balance

    How net radiation (Rn) is partitioned into latent heat (LE), sensible heat (H) and ground heat flux (G), and how that partitioning shapes land-surface warming and drying, including data-driven corrections for energy-balance closure in flux networks.

    • Rn, LE, H, G
    • Data-driven closure correction
    • FLUXNET

  • Wildfire and post-fire carbon recovery

    How burned ecosystems across North America recover their carbon uptake, and what controls the pace of that recovery.

    • Disturbance
    • Carbon recovery
    • North America
